Feeds

Follow Threat Radar in your feed reader, Slack or automation tool. Paste a feed address and new items show up as they're published.

Main feeds

  • Newly exploited vulnerabilities

    The 50 newest entries in CISA's catalog of vulnerabilities attackers are actively using. Updated twice a day.

  • Cyber Sitrep

    The daily situation report, every weekday morning: TL;DR, number of items, and whether action is needed.

  • Breach Wire

    Daily ransomware leak-site statistics: claimed victims, countries, groups, and whether Sweden was hit. Never victim names.

Just your stack

This downloads a small subscription file (OPML) with one feed per ticked vendor. Import it in your feed reader, usually under "Import OPML". The file is made here in your browser; the site never sees your stack. Once you're subscribed, your feed reader fetches those vendor feeds, so its service can see which ones you follow.

Vendor feeds

One feed per vendor, with that vendor's 20 newest exploited vulnerabilities.

    Can't find a product? CISA files some products under the parent company (VMware is under Broadcom). The My stack picker on the Radar searches product names too.

    How to use them

    Feed readers

    Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire, Thunderbird and most others: add a feed and paste an address. Many find all three main feeds from just the site address.

    Slack

    Add Slack's RSS app to your workspace, then in a channel type /feed subscribe followed by a feed address.

    Automation tools

    Zapier ("RSS by Zapier → New item in feed"), n8n ("RSS Feed Trigger") and Power Automate ("RSS → When a feed item is published"): paste a feed address into the trigger, then send the items wherever you like.

    Feed addresses stay the same over time. Items keep permanent IDs, so tools never post the same item twice. If CISA renames a vendor, that vendor's old feed stops updating.

    Data: CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog (US government, public domain). Not affiliated with CISA.
